the idea →
Closest to your Rocksky reference. A familiar discovery grid where the B/F/F badge rides on each card instead of “recently played.” Leads with people you follow, back-fills with recent tags. Fast to scan — but the three-way idea stays implicit.
Makes the concept literal — three live streams of Best / Favorite / First side by side. Strongest identity; you instantly “get” the app. Less room per item; can feel busy on smaller screens.
Social & narrative — full sentences, the tagger's note, and reactions. Best when the feed is really about the people you follow. Lower density: fewer albums per screen.
Opinionated “set your three.” Drag an album from the discography into a labelled slot; one album per slot, swap anytime. Great for first setup — drag can be fiddly for quick edits.
Quickest edits — tag any album inline. Tags are exclusive, so re-tagging just moves the badge. A sticky bar shows your current trio. Three buttons × N albums can get noisy.
A calm 3-question flow — perfect for onboarding or an empty artist. Note how “First” is framed as a recommendation to newcomers. Slower if you just want to change one pick.
AT-proto means sign in with a handle on any server. A keeps it dead simple (handle first, advanced server link tucked away); B surfaces server choice up-front for self-hosters. Real flow redirects to the user's PDS for OAuth — no password stored here.
Your library as trios — one Best / Favorite / First per artist. Empty slots (see Daft Punk) invite you to complete the set. Mirrors how you tag, artist by artist.
Grouped by tag instead — scan everything you've named Best, then Favorite, then First. Good for sharing “here are all my starter picks.”
